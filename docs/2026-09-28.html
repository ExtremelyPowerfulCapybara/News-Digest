El rendimiento del Treasury a 10 años alcanza máximos de 19 años: qué lo está impulsando
El rendimiento del bono del Tesoro estadounidense a 10 años trepó al 5.23% esta semana, su nivel más alto desde 2007, con el mercado de futuros asignando una probabilidad del 64% a una suba de tasas en octubre. El factor determinante no es solo la inflación persistente sino el volumen récord de emisión de deuda — corporativa y soberana — para financiar infraestructura de inteligencia artificial, lo que comprime la demanda de Treasuries y eleva los rendimientos.
El salto del rendimiento a 10 años desde 4.8% a principios de mes hasta 5.23% en pocos días es inusualmente abrupto y coincide con una subasta débil de bonos a 5 años, lo que sugiere que el problema de absorción de deuda se está acelerando.

10-year Treasury yield hits 19-year high: what's driving it
The 10-year U.S. Treasury yield climbed to 5.23% this week, its highest since 2007, with futures markets pricing a 64% probability of a rate hike in October. The key driver is not just persistent inflation but a record wave of debt issuance — both corporate and sovereign — to finance artificial intelligence infrastructure, crowding out Treasury demand and pushing yields higher.
The jump in the 10-year yield from 4.8% earlier this month to 5.23% in just days is unusually abrupt and coincides with a weak 5-year Treasury auction, suggesting the debt absorption problem is accelerating.

Argentina: poverty at 32.3%, economic activity falls 2.9%, country risk closes at 609 bps
Poverty rose to 32.3%, unemployment stood at 7.9% in Q2 — above the 7.6% recorded a year earlier — and Argentina's monthly economic activity index fell 2.9% in July, with trade, industry, and construction all in negative territory. Against this backdrop, the government is debating internally whether to adopt stimulus measures without compromising fiscal balance, while country risk at 609 basis points keeps Argentina locked out of international debt markets.
This week's global yield surge hit Argentina disproportionately: while most Latin American country risk spreads fell, Argentina's rose roughly 200 points since early July, worsening its refinancing costs.

Acciones de consumo en China atrapadas en una década perdida mientras el boom de IA concentra flujos

China consumer stocks trapped in a lost decade as AI boom concentrates flows
Weak spending, declining disposable income, and falling property prices keep Chinese consumer companies depressed, as capital flows shift toward the technology and AI sector. The result is an internal bifurcation of the Chinese market that mirrors, at scale, the concentration seen in the U.S. Nasdaq: a handful of tech winners and a majority of lagging sectors.
