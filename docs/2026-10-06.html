Treasuries above 5%: fiscal apocalypse or signal of a resilient economy?
The 10-year Treasury yield is now firmly above 5% while the U.S. government's net interest costs reached approximately $1.05 trillion in the first eleven months of fiscal year 2026. TD Securities warns that financing costs could escalate to $1.4 trillion in 2027 if rates remain elevated, though it notes that the weighted-average debt maturity of 5.9 years acts as a temporary buffer. The trap lies in the cycle: debt generates interest, interest generates more debt; those calling it 'exaggerated' are right until the day they aren't.
September's near-flat payroll number raised odds of a Fed hold in October, but Wednesday's meeting minutes will be the real test of how seriously the committee is weighing the fiscal feedback loop.

China closes 670 banks in 2025: the largest purge of its financial system in recent history
Beijing shuttered a record 670 banking institutions — mostly rural — in 2025, equivalent to one quarter of the country's banks, in a forced consolidation drive aimed at creating larger and better-capitalized entities, according to Fitch Ratings. Rural banks show nonperforming loan ratios of 2.8% versus the sector average of 1.5%, return on assets that fell to 0.45%, and concentrated exposure to property developers and local government funding vehicles — the exact triangle no regulator wants to see together.
China's GDP grew just 4.3% in Q2 — its slowest since 2022 — meaning this banking consolidation is less a prudential policy choice and more an emergency response to a system absorbing simultaneous real estate stress and weakening domestic demand.

Crédito argentino: récord en dólares pero contracción real en pesos revela economía de dos velocidades
El stock de crédito en dólares al sector privado superó los 26.230 millones de dólares en septiembre —acumulando una expansión del 41% en 2026— mientras el crédito en pesos creció apenas 1,45% nominal en el mes, por debajo de una inflación estimada entre 1,7% y 1,9%, con la Base Monetaria contrayéndose 16% en términos reales respecto a hace un año. El patrón es nítido: los sectores exportadores con acceso a divisas —energía, minería, agro— se financian y expanden, mientras industria, comercio y construcción operan en un entorno de crédito que se achica en términos reales y 5,8 millones de argentinos han quedado fuera del sistema bancario por el deterioro de la morosidad.
El IPC oficial de septiembre se publicará el martes 13, y si confirma inflación por encima del crecimiento del crédito en pesos, quedará validada la paradoja Milei: el apretón monetario que prometía ser transitorio lleva más de un año contrayendo el crédito real mientras el modelo exportador avanza sobre sus propios rieles.

Argentine credit: dollar record but real peso contraction reveals a two-speed economy
Dollar credit to the private sector exceeded $26.23 billion in September — accumulating 41% expansion in 2026 — while peso credit grew just 1.45% nominally in the month, below estimated inflation of 1.7%-1.9%, with the monetary base contracting 16% in real terms versus a year ago. The pattern is clear: export-oriented sectors with access to foreign currency — energy, mining, agriculture — borrow and expand, while industry, commerce, and construction operate in an environment of shrinking real credit, and 5.8 million Argentines have been locked out of the banking system by deteriorating loan quality.
Argentina's official September CPI publishes Tuesday the 13th — if it confirms inflation above peso credit growth, it will validate the Milei paradox: a monetary squeeze meant to be temporary has now contracted real credit for over a year while the export-led model runs on a separate track entirely.

La resistencia ciudadana frena 42.000 millones en inversiones en centros de datos en Europa

Con los precios de electricidad en Europa ya estructuralmente más altos que en EE.UU. y la demanda energética de los centros de datos creciendo exponencialmente, el rechazo regulatorio añade una prima de riesgo política que los modelos de valoración del sector tecnológico todavía no incorporan plenamente.
