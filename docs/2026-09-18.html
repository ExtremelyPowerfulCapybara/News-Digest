El alza llega después de que el IPC de agosto sorprendiera al alza, borrando las esperanzas de verano de que la inflación convergía al objetivo; el mercado ahora cotiza más de dos subidas adicionales para finales de 2027.

Fed raises rates for first time in three years; Warsh defies Trump's political mandate
Kevin Warsh presided over a unanimous decision to raise borrowing costs by 25 basis points — the first hike since July 2023 — citing annual inflation of 3.4% still well above the 2% target. Most Fed officials signaled further increases in 2026 and 2027, putting Warsh on a direct collision course with the president who appointed him to do precisely the opposite.
The hike follows an August CPI surprise that erased summer hopes of inflation converging toward target; markets are now pricing in more than two additional increases through end-2027.
