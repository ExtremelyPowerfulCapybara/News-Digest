Analistas advierten que los rebotes de un solo día desde condiciones de sobreventa extrema aparecen tanto en mercados bajistas prolongados como en suelos definitivos, y que el índice sigue un 3% por debajo de su media móvil de 20 días.
El S&P cotiza hoy en 7.201, aún por debajo del nivel de 7.300 que frenó el rally del lunes según los datos de mercado disponibles, lo que da peso a la tesis de que el rebote fue técnico y no un cambio de tendencia.

Does the Nvidia-led rip mark the market bottom? Maybe not
The S&P 500's 4.7% surge on Monday was driven by more than a third by Nvidia's 31% jump following Jensen Huang's reinstatement, but the index closed right at technical resistance corresponding to its pre-breakdown summertime low. Analysts warn that single-day rebounds from deeply oversold conditions appear inside prolonged bear markets as often as they mark definitive bottoms, and the index remains about 3% below its 20-day moving average.
The S&P currently trades at 7,201, still below the 7,300 level that capped Monday's rally according to available market data, lending weight to the thesis that the bounce was technical rather than a trend reversal.

Some U.S. food banks see demand top pandemic levels
Organizations like West Side Campaign Against Hunger in New York expect to distribute as much as 6 million pounds of food this year, with demand surpassing pandemic peaks; 13.7% of US households — roughly 18.3 million — experienced food insecurity in 2024, the highest rate since 2015.
