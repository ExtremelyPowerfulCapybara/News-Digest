Binance clients withdraw $5 billion in three days following the largest crypto hack in history
Binance users have withdrawn up to $5 billion (€4.46 billion) since Sunday, when the platform gradually resumed services after a $6.3 billion theft carried out early Friday morning, attributed by the FBI to North Korean hacker group TraderTraitor. The company says it has covered approximately 95% of the affected amount using its own revenues and an emergency fund, but the capital flight—nearly 3% of assets under custody—lays bare the fragility of institutional confidence in centralized exchanges.
Bitcoin is up 1.1% to $78,171 and Ethereum gains 1.9% to $2,316 today, suggesting the broader crypto market has not entered systemic panic, though the scale of the hack—the largest in sector history—and the speed of Binance outflows raise serious questions about centralized custody.
