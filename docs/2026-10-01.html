Bonds accumulate historic losses and offer the best entry point in decades, analysts say
The iShares Core U.S. Aggregate Bond ETF (AGG) has fallen 4.8% in price this year with a negative total return of 2.2%, while the S&P 500 advances 14%, widening the gap to 15 percentage points in favor of equities over the past decade. The quarterly rebalancing that favors bonds is approaching, but institutional flows are scarce: investors fear fixed income as if it were equity with a coupon.

Exfuncionario de la Fed condenado por pasar información sensible a espías chinos durante más de una década
John Harold Rogers, economista senior de la Reserva Federal, fue arrestado en enero de 2025 y condenado en 2026 por filtrar documentos internos del banco central a un agente del gobierno chino que conoció en 2013 en Shanghái, según una investigación de CNBC con acceso a mensajes, grabaciones y evidencia del juicio. El caso expone vulnerabilidades de contrainteligencia dentro de la institución que fija la política monetaria más influyente del mundo, justo cuando las tensiones EE.UU.-China escalan en frentes comerciales y tecnológicos.
La condena ocurre mientras la Fed delibera sobre política monetaria en un entorno de inflación persistente; cualquier sospecha de filtración de información privilegiada sobre decisiones de tasas tiene implicaciones directas para la integridad de los mercados.

Former Fed official convicted of passing sensitive information to Chinese spies for over a decade
John Harold Rogers, a senior Federal Reserve economist, was arrested in January 2025 and convicted in 2026 for leaking internal central bank documents to a Chinese government agent he met in 2013 in Shanghai, according to a CNBC investigation with access to messages, recordings, and trial evidence. The case exposes counterintelligence vulnerabilities within the institution that sets the world's most influential monetary policy, just as U.S.-China tensions escalate on trade and technology fronts.
The conviction comes as the Fed deliberates on monetary policy amid persistent inflation; any suspicion of leaked privileged information about rate decisions has direct implications for market integrity.
