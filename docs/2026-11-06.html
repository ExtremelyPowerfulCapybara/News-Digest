El Banco de Inglaterra sube tipos al 4% por primera vez desde 2023 con la inflación en 3,5%
El Comité de Política Monetaria del Banco de Inglaterra votó 8 a 1 para elevar el tipo oficial del 3,75% al 4%, la primera subida desde agosto de 2023, en respuesta a una inflación del 3,5% alimentada en parte por el encarecimiento del petróleo desde el inicio de la guerra entre EE.UU. e Israel contra Irán. Bank of England raises rates to 4% for first time since 2023 as inflation sits at 3.5%
The Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee voted 8-to-1 to raise the benchmark rate from 3.75% to 4%, the first hike since August 2023, as inflation running at 3.5% — partly fueled by oil prices since the U.S.-Israel war against Iran began — made inaction untenable. Governor Andrew Bailey cautioned the move is not "the first in a long inevitable sequence," but warned that absent a resolution to the Middle East conflict, it is unlikely to be the last.
The 10Y UST yields 4.84% today and the VIX is up 38.7% on the week; the Bank of England's move reinforces the narrative of global central banks still tightening while Brent crude holds near $99.

Binance's $6.3 billion hack prompts Fed and OCC to check bank systems for vulnerabilities
Supervisors from the Federal Reserve and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency reached out to major U.S. banks on Monday to probe for unusual activity or disruptions following last week's $6.3 billion Binance hack, believed to be the largest financial heist in history. Banks are conducting internal diagnostics and at least one of the largest U.S. lenders dispatched a cybersecurity team to coordinate directly with Binance, amid concerns that rapidly evolving AI models are outpacing banks' defenses.

Hong Kong passes law requiring crypto platforms to report user tax data to foreign authorities from 2028
Hong Kong's Legislative Council approved an amendment incorporating the OECD's Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) and the updated Common Reporting Standard, requiring digital asset platforms to begin collecting user data in 2027 and automatically exchanging it with other tax jurisdictions from 2028. Financial Services Secretary Christopher Hui argued the measure is "crucial" to maintaining Hong Kong's reputation as an international financial center and shedding any image as an opaque haven for digital capital.
The news comes days after the $6.3 billion Binance hack put U.S. regulators on alert; Hong Kong's move is part of a global regulatory wave compressing crypto anonymity just as Bitcoin trades at $76,207.

Nippon Steel raised its net profit forecast for the fiscal year ending March 2027 from 290 billion yen to 440 billion yen ($2.8 billion), with US Steel alone expected to contribute 230 billion yen to underlying business profit, up 50 billion yen from prior estimates, CFO Takahiko Iwai told a press conference. India propone eliminar el impuesto sobre ganancias de capital para inversores extranjeros, pero los expertos dudan de su eficacia
El regulador bursátil indio instó al gobierno a eliminar el impuesto sobre las ganancias de capital a largo plazo (LTCG) para los inversores extranjeros, después de que un informe de Nomura revelara que la participación foránea en bolsa ha caído a un mínimo histórico del 14%; la ministra de Finanzas, Nirmala Sitharaman, reconoció las salidas de capital y dijo que estudiaría la revisión del impuesto. India floats LTCG tax cut to woo back foreign investors, but experts say it won't be enough
India's capital market regulator has urged the government to scrap the long-term capital gains (LTCG) tax for foreign portfolio investors, as foreign holdings of Indian stocks have fallen to an all-time low of 14%, according to a Nomura report; Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman acknowledged the outflows and said she would consider revising the tax. Portfolio manager Amit Bhartia of Delorean Partners cautioned that "a tax cut helps at the margin, but allocators tend to buy expected returns, not tax breaks," noting that the perceived relative return — not the tax rate — drove the record outflow in October 2024.
The MSCI EM is down 5.8% today, reflecting broad pressure on emerging markets; India also faces higher energy prices and a rupee struggling to stabilize at 94.46 against the dollar, according to BusinessLine data.
