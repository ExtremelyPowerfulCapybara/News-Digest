FMI alerta: 2027 será un año de deuda récord, inflación persistente y riesgos de IA

El discurso de Georgieva llega días antes de las reuniones anuales FMI/Banco Mundial en Bangkok y coincide con el Brent superando $102 y los bonos del Tesoro retrocediendo, dotando de urgencia concreta a lo que podría haberse leído como retórica de apertura.

IMF warns: 2027 will bring record debt, persistent inflation, and AI risks
IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva warned on Wednesday that the global economy faces a convergence in 2027 of record-high public debt, bond yields driven up by two active wars, and emerging risks from artificial intelligence investment. Georgieva urged central banks to maintain a 'prudently hawkish' bias, praised recent rate hikes by the Fed, ECB, and Bank of Japan, and pressed governments to make 'very tough political choices' rather than waiting for growth to resolve their fiscal imbalances.
Georgieva's speech comes days before the annual IMF/World Bank meetings in Bangkok and coincides with Brent topping $102 and Treasuries selling off, giving concrete urgency to what might otherwise read as opening-ceremony rhetoric.

Ray Dalio: the cushion protecting equities from rising yields is shrinking
Bridgewater Associates founder Ray Dalio warned from the Milken Institute Asia Summit in Singapore that stocks have weathered the global bond sell-off thanks to earnings growth, but that relative advantage is narrowing as share prices climb and yields rise — and that credit spreads are already beginning to widen as an early warning signal. Dalio said he expects free cash flows to deteriorate even if reported earnings keep improving, and reiterated his conviction that the bond bear market has 'more to go.'
Dalio's free cash flow warning lands just ahead of third-quarter earnings season, when the market will be under pressure to justify elevated valuations in a high-rate environment.

CNBC Comercio
El NY Fed confirma: los aranceles de Trump encarecieron bienes cotidianos 2.9 puntos porcentuales
Un equipo de investigadores de la Reserva Federal de Nueva York determinó que los aranceles de Trump elevaron el costo de 67 categorías de bienes en 2.9 puntos porcentuales a febrero de este año, y que sin los gravámenes los precios habrían caído casi 1%; el impacto inflacionario se extenderá hasta 2027, según el estudio. Alrededor de dos tercios del efecto vino directamente de los aranceles y el tercio restante de efectos indirectos — como fabricantes estadounidenses que usan insumos importados —, contradiciendo la narrativa de la Casa Blanca de que los exportadores extranjeros absorberían el costo.
El estudio del NY Fed es la evidencia cuantitativa más detallada publicada hasta ahora sobre el impacto arancelario en consumidores, y llega en un momento en que la Corte Suprema ya anuló parte de los aranceles pero la Casa Blanca busca reimponerlos por vías alternativas.

NY Fed confirms: Trump's tariffs raised everyday goods prices by 2.9 percentage points
A team of researchers at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York found that Trump's tariffs raised the cost of 67 goods categories by 2.9 percentage points as of February, and that without the levies prices for those products would have fallen nearly 1%; the inflationary impact is expected to persist through 2027, according to the study. About two-thirds of the effect came directly from the tariffs themselves and one-third from knock-on effects — such as U.S. manufacturers using imported inputs — directly contradicting the White House's narrative that foreign exporters would absorb the cost.
The NY Fed study is the most detailed quantitative evidence yet on the tariff impact on consumers, arriving as the Supreme Court has already struck down some levies but the White House seeks to reimpose them through alternative mechanisms.

Venezuelan crude deliveries to India are expected to more than double in October to approximately 465,000 barrels per day, up from 196,000 in September, driven by Reliance Industries capitalizing on discounts available in sanctioned oil. El Financiero México
México aprueba reforma de nacionalidad única para aspirantes a cargos ejecutivos con voto esencialmente partidista
La reforma constitucional que exige nacionalidad exclusivamente mexicana para postularse a la Presidencia, gubernaturas o la Jefatura de Gobierno de la CDMX fue ratificada por 22 congresos estatales — 21 gobernados por Morena y uno por el PVEM —, con los estados fronterizos de oposición (Chihuahua, Coahuila y Nuevo León) ausentes del proceso. La aprobación siguió líneas partidistas de forma casi perfecta — 344 a 116 en Diputados, 86 a 42 en el Senado — revelando que la reforma fue un ejercicio de cohesión de la mayoría gobernante más que una deliberación federalista sobre soberanía y migración.
La reforma entra en vigor en 2028, justo antes del siguiente ciclo electoral federal, y su aprobación centralizada vía mayoría Morena plantea preguntas sobre el alcance real del federalismo mexicano en decisiones constitucionales de alto impacto político.

Mexico approves single-nationality reform for executive office candidates in an essentially partisan vote
The constitutional reform requiring exclusively Mexican nationality to run for the Presidency, state governorships, or Mexico City's chief executive post was ratified by 22 state legislatures — 21 governed by Morena and one by the PVEM — with opposition-governed border states Chihuahua, Coahuila, and Nuevo León absent from the process. Approval followed partisan lines almost perfectly — 344 to 116 in the Chamber of Deputies, 86 to 42 in the Senate — revealing that the reform was an exercise in ruling-majority cohesion rather than a federalist deliberation on sovereignty and migration.
The reform takes effect in 2028, just ahead of the next federal electoral cycle, and its centralized approval via the Morena majority raises questions about the real scope of Mexican federalism in constitutionally high-stakes political decisions.
