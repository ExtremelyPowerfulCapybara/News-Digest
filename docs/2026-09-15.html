Saudi Arabia shuts East-West pipeline after drone attacks; Brent rises to $108
Satellite images confirm severe damage to Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline, which has a capacity of 7 million barrels per day, closed as a precautionary measure following multiple drone attacks launched from Iraq, pushing Brent crude to $108.02 a barrel. The closure eliminates the primary alternative corridor to the Strait of Hormuz, leaving global crude markets without a safety net just as Houthi forces also threaten the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.
The East-West pipeline was the logistical buffer preventing the Hormuz closure from becoming a total supply shock; its shutdown transforms a contained crisis into an energy emergency with no immediate alternative routes.
